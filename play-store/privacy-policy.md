# Tidy Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-07

Tidy Launcher is designed to organize your Android home screen without sending your app inventory or layout data to external servers.

## What Tidy Launcher accesses

- Installed app metadata needed to build the launcher experience
- App labels and package names
- App icons for local color grouping
- Local launcher settings you choose inside the app

## What Tidy Launcher does not do

- No account creation
- No analytics
- No advertising SDKs
- No cloud sync
- No sale of personal data
- No sharing of installed app data with third parties

## How data is processed

All app classification, color grouping, recommendation logic, and layout generation are performed locally on the device.

## Data retention

Launcher settings and app organization metadata stay on the device until the app is removed or its local storage is cleared.

## Children

Tidy Launcher does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
